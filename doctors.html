Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our doctors and medical services.

How can I find the right doctor for my condition?
You can browse our doctors by specialty or use our search feature to find the right specialist. Our patient care team is also available to help you choose the best doctor for your needs.
How do I book an appointment with a doctor?
You can book an appointment online through our appointment booking form, call our helpline, or visit our hospital in person. We also offer telemedicine consultations for convenience.
Are all doctors board-certified?
Yes, all our doctors are board-certified in their respective specialties and have undergone rigorous training and education from recognized medical institutions.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring your ID, insurance card, any relevant medical records, and a list of current medications. If you have any test results from other facilities, please bring those as well.
Can I see a doctor without insurance?
Yes, we offer flexible payment options and self-pay rates for patients without insurance. Our billing department can help you understand the costs and payment options available.
Is telemedicine consultation available?
Yes, we offer comprehensive telemedicine services for consultation, follow-ups, and routine check-ups. You can connect with our doctors from the comfort of your home through video or phone consultations.
