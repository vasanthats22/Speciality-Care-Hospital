Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Specialty Care Hospital services, appointments, and facilities.

What medical specialties does Specialty Care Hospital offer?
We offer a wide range of medical specialties including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, General Medicine, Gynecology, Dermatology, and Emergency Care. All our departments are staffed by highly qualified specialists.
How can I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by calling our helpline, using our online booking system, or visiting our hospital in person. We also offer telemedicine consultations for patients who prefer virtual visits.
Does the hospital accept health insurance?
Yes, we accept all major health insurance plans. Our billing department will help you verify your coverage and process insurance claims. We also offer flexible payment options for uninsured patients.
What emergency services are available?
Our 24/7 emergency department is fully equipped to handle all types of medical emergencies. We have a dedicated trauma team, ambulances, and emergency surgery facilities available round the clock.
How do I get my test results?
Test results are available through our patient portal, by visiting our hospital, or by calling our lab department. Results are typically available within 24-48 hours. Your doctor will also discuss the results with you during your follow-up appointment.
Do you offer telemedicine services?
Yes! We offer comprehensive telemedicine services for consultation, follow-ups, and routine check-ups. You can connect with our doctors from the comfort of your home through video or phone consultations.
