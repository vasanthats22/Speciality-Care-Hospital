Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about appointments, services, and facilities at Specialty Care Hospital.

What medical specialties does Specialty Care Hospital offer?
We offer a wide range of medical specialties including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Dermatology, General Medicine, and Emergency Care. All our departments are staffed by highly qualified specialists.
How can I book an appointment online?
Simply fill out the booking form on this page with your details, select a department and date, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation within 24 hours via email or SMS.
Can I choose my preferred doctor?
Yes! You can browse our doctors section and select your preferred specialist based on their expertise, experience, and availability.
What happens after I book an appointment?
After booking, our team will review your request and send you a confirmation email or SMS with the appointment details, doctor information, and any additional instructions.
Does the hospital accept health insurance?
Yes, we accept all major health insurance plans. Our billing department will help you verify your coverage and process insurance claims. We also offer flexible payment options for uninsured patients.
Is telemedicine available for appointments?
Yes! We offer comprehensive telemedicine services for consultation, follow-ups, and routine check-ups. You can connect with our doctors from the comfort of your home through video or phone consultations.
