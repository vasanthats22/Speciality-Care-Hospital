Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our medical specialties and services.

What specialties does Specialty Care Hospital offer?
We offer a wide range of medical specialties including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Dermatology, General Medicine, and Emergency Care. All our departments are staffed by highly qualified specialists.
How do I know which specialty I need?
You can consult with our primary care physicians who will evaluate your condition and refer you to the appropriate specialist. You can also browse our specialties and contact us directly for guidance.
Do you have specialists for children?
Yes, we have a dedicated Pediatrics department with specialized doctors for children from infancy through adolescence. We provide comprehensive pediatric care including vaccinations, growth monitoring, and treatment of childhood illnesses.
What advanced technology do you use?
We use state-of-the-art medical technology including Digital X-Ray, ECG & Echo, advanced laboratory diagnostics, and robotic surgery systems for minimally invasive procedures.
How can I book an appointment with a specialist?
You can book an appointment online through our appointment booking form, call our helpline, or visit our hospital in person. We also offer telemedicine consultations for convenience.
